Call of the Crystals decks - Version 1.5

Gwen - The Bride: This allied Gwen has been tested, tuned, retested, tossed aside, brought back, rebuilt, and had some of the best minds in Shadow Era give me their input. Tournament ready.

Zaladar - Crystal Miner: My baby. I’ve had this deck almost as long as 1.5 has been out. My favourite QM deck. A modified version took me from 0 to 323 in the Season 5 reset before I switched it out.

Majiya - Hybrid: This is a record breaker, netting me a 7 win streak in an internal King-of-the-Hill tournament. Very solid, very flexible. An excellent QM deck, and quite capable in tournament play.

Darkclaw – Wulven Forces: My surprise deck, and always fun to take out for a spin. An allied Darkclaw that gives people more to think about than the traditional SoloClaw.

Ter Adun - The Spartan: The result of my own raving insanity, this is probably as good as I think Ter can be, winning matches in the 300+ range. Warning: Not for the faint hearted!

Logan - Metabreaker: Of all the heroes, Logan fell the hardest in the balancing of 1.5. Time to bring him back

Eladwen - Hardened Rush: This is the deck that got me into the Top 100 in Season 5, climbing from 295 to 303 and #42 in the rankings. A very solid, no frills, ass kicking rush deck. (Hosted on the Shadow Era Network)
