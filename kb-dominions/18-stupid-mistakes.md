## 18.1 My personal mistakes

### 18.1.1 Learned from YARG2 playing as EA R'lyeh

```text
- Never cast twiceborn on amphibian units or pretender when capital is UW. If he dies and turns into a wight he will drown
- UW you want friendly current for nearly every battle possible
- Don't take an awake research rainbow pretender. It failed the real test for a number of reasons:
-- Really hurt early expansion -> neighbors attacked R'lyeh mistaking it for an easy target
-- sacrifice dominion -> needed for autospwaning 

- Instead take something like Dom 10, good scales pathless Wyrm
```

### 18.1.2 Learned from Sharivar1 playing as LA Ermor

```text
- Rainbow master lich with small multiple bless works well
- Possible to take W9D4 pretender for awesome bless to lictors (who can also be summoned en mass)
- Good call to make starting lictor prophet and thug him up
- Summon tarts for magic diversity
```

### 18.1.3 Learned from YARG1 playing as LA Bogarus

```text
- E4 Wyrm did no good. Take pathless Wyrm instead.
- Blood blood blood. Focus on blood eco and spells.
- Consider to take good scales G3P3Co3L0M1
- Don't over research or summon/forge fancy stuff while at war. If at war make sure to use everything available in order to win it
```

### 18.1.4 Learned from LAND RAND playing as MA Machaka

```text
- Rainbow lich turned out to be useless. Take awake Wyrm instead.
- Get flaming arrows ASAP
- Consider medium to major bless for spider hunters
```

### 18.1.5 Learned from AsiaTwist playing as MA Jotunheim

```text
- N9 bless is important, breserk reduce routs and saves battles
- E9N9 is the bless to go
- Give shroud to shapeshifters

```

## 18.2 Common mistakes

```text
From wiki: http://dom3.servegame.com/wiki/Common_mistakes

Ideas
Not contacting other players/nations but staying silent in MP games, diplomacy is key

Pre-game
Not working out a long term strategy before the game begins
Not taking an awake combat pretender when playing a nation with weak national troops.
Not taking the time to work out a pretender design with a long term strategy in mind.
Not raising Dominion to "safe levels", but only picking strong magics (newbie mistake)

Early game
Not expanding fast enough
Recruiting low quality indep troops to spend extra gold
Attacking strong independents without a good plan

Movement
When attacking a province, sneaking into a province with some of your commanders by mistake (can be avoided by ctrl+clicking to move)
Not accounting for water-based movement onto land
Not taking into account an enemy's ability to sail or fly or cloud trapeze or teleport
Not knowing all the neighbor connections from a province

Provincial Defense
If you do not buy any PD, it's easy for an opponent to take a whole bunch of provinces from you just using scouts. Buy at least 1PD everywhere
If you spend too much on PD you'll waste gold that could be better put to use expanding

Combat
Failure to decoy archers (use of a few troops in front and to the of others to draw fire)
Forgetting to separate troops with harmful auras (heat, poison, chill) from their vulnerable allies
Getting your super combatant commanders killed by forgetting to script them properly
Not providing an avenue of retreat for your army
```
