QUOTE (fPickle @ Oct 21 2011, 08:47 PM)
Regarding fortresses....is it most beneficial to make them everywhere? Will they eventually increase my income in all my cities? Or should I place them in strategic locations on my map? For the maximum benefit, how should they be built?

Fortresses offer five benefits.
1) Lets you recruit national troops and commanders.
2) Doubles the number of resources in the province and pulls them from neighboring, friendly, fort-less provinces.
3) Increases income in the province by admin/2%
4) Increases number of supplies in nearby provinces
5) Makes the province harder to capture: requiring an army to tear down the fort walls and slay any defenders within to truly claim the province.


QUOTE (fPickle @ Oct 21 2011, 08:47 PM)
Regarding mages and magic level. If I have mages that have access to fire, and the highest mage I can make has fire level 4. To get higher levels in that type of magic, do I have to spend gems to level him up? Does this basically make it possible to have all types of magic in every game...as long as there's enough gems?

Yes, given an unlimited amount of gems and time, you could turn the lowliest scout into the greatest mage. However, there is rarely so much time and never so enough gems to do this. The most cost-effective way is to build path boosting items (http://forum.shrapnelgames.com/showthread.php?t=37423). It costs 45 gems to empower a E2 mage to an E3 but it only costs 10 gems to forge earth boots which can boost a E2 to an E3. Then that boosted E2 can summon an E3 Troll King, hand him the boots so the player now has an E4.

Very few nations natively have access to all 8 magical paths. Most nations can summon units to expand the number of paths they have casters in. Then of course there is your pretender, which has whatever paths you gave it. However, you don't NEED all 8 to succeed. If you are a nation which heavily focuses on fire and astral magic, it might be a more efficient use of your gems, research and mage-turns sharpening your fire and astral knives rather than breaking into water. EA T'ien Ch'i can access every path save blood whereas EA Caelum has an overwhelming preponderance towards air magic. That said, Caelum is a fully competitive nation despite, or possibly because, of their magical focus. A specialist can trump a generalist if they play to their strengths.


QUOTE (fPickle @ Oct 21 2011, 08:47 PM)
Regarding supplies. I keep running into a situation where I have to hold a province with low supplies. And at the same time I need to launch my attack from that province. I end up having starving and diseased troops that do not do so well. I've tried building fortresses in that province but my supply problem still persists.

Units use up Size minus 1 supplies each turn or they begin starving. Some units don't eat (this is especially true of summoned units), other units eat more than they should (this is especially true of Yomi and Hinnom). A nature mage creates 10 supplies per level of nature magic they possess. Also there are a number of nature items which create supplies. Finally, your forts increase the supplies of your provinces based on their admin value and the distance from the province. Therefore a Great City fort (admin 50) will create a lot of supplies in and around them, whereas an admin 0 swamp fort won't produce any.

It's easy enough to save your game and go back. Just copy the directory in dominions3\savedgames\mygame to another name. I tend to use say mygameturn24 to make it easy.

But take note: This game was intended to be multiplayer from it's original design. The single player experience is okay but not amazing. The AI is pretty weak by mid to late game because it does REALLY stupid things. And it's a sure thing Illwinter isn't going to do much about it. Multiplayer is where the real action occurs. So a save feature is both not required and fundamentally bad for multiplayer.

Some of your other questions:

1) The nation guides on the wiki or on Shrapnel are good as "dummy" guides because of the nature of the game. If you learn a nation you can then extrapolate to other nations with similar strategies.

There are some specific guides on particular strategies. Have a look here on Shrapnel:
Strategy Index
No one has bothered with a general purpose beginner guide because it would really be too huge and probably bad advice. What works for one nation is bad for another.

2) Forts: Most nations guides include a recommended fort section for the nation. Every nation has different needs for forts. My normal rule of thumb is at least 3 forts to recruit magi. Others depend on the situation.

3) Experience: There are only 4? levels of experience. Each gives +1 att, def, and a bit other. Can't quite remember. But most important are the gold stars for heroic ability. Those are much better than experience. Commanders get them for getting into the hall of fame and get better if they stay in the hall of fame. There is a list somewhere. Try searching the wiki for Heroic.

4) Magi and boosting. There is a very handy chart in the back of the manual listing all magic boosters.

5) I think you got good answers here. There are 3 large dom3 communities and some smaller. Here, Shrapnel, and Something Awful. In order of number of games to join I think it's SA, Shrapnel, then here.

6) Mods are installed by unzipping them to \dominions3\mods and then going to Dominions and enabling them. You probably figured that out. Some mods conflict and that causes the error. Nothing you as a user can do about that except don't use enable conflicting mods at the same time. Doesn't matter if you install them. Just don't enable. For how to avoid conflicting mods, I guess there are some basic rules.
- only use one balance mod at a time unless the mod author specifically tells you what other mods can work together.
- if you want to try nations mods, use the ones from the mod compatibility project in our mods section. They have been made specifically to work together and with CBM. Look at the stickies on the mods forum for more help there. If you have specific mods you want to try together post a question there. The modders will be sure to help.

7) I think you got the answer here. Illwinter did not provide any ways to mod the UI. For the graphics, there is a lot being done in the mods forum and the newer mods have new sprites. However, the underlying graphic engine can't really be affected. Personally it doesn't matter in the slightest. I don't play Dom3 for it's graphical beauty. I play it because it's the greatest 4x game ever smile.gif Well at least for multiplayer. For single player I don't play Dom3.

As far as I know the only way to mod the UI would be to reverse engineer the turn files. However, that runs a huge risk of allowing cheating and so has been generally avoided. If you can query the turn files to get more info you can pretty easily hack them and give yourself free resources or whatever. It's been done and is the basest form of cheating.

8) You got your answer here. All increased difficulty does is give the AI more gold. It never gets any smarter and just keeps repeating the same tactics of masses of chaff troops. The AI doesn't even know to ensure it sends priests to bless it's sacreds.

If you really want a better AI experience there are some threads on Shrapnel about mods and tricks to make it better. Google for SemiRandom and "Better AI". It helps a lot to use the switch site:forum.shrapnelgames.com in google. SemiRandom is an external utility that sets up viable pretenders for the AI and can give it better starting armies and properly geared commanders. It can't do anything about the game after it starts. It just provides the AI with a better start. The base AI makes really bad pretenders that do stupid things like go to the arena and get killed or have huge blesses but still don't send priests. Another good mod for the AI is Better Independents. It removes a bunch of the really terrible chaff units so the AI will recruit it's better units.

	
QUOTE (joker_mp @ Jun 17 2012, 08:18 AM)
So, i understand a role of DRN in melee, distant combat and in magic resistance. What about actual damage from an spells like agony or from hight death bless - is it always the same? I think so, but just to be sure.
Will my standard attacks be magic too if i have a secondary magic attack from hight bless? Or i must bypass 75% fail chance of ethereal to make a hit and only then secondary attack will be applied?
Do effects of two "Bane venom charm" stack if placed on one unit? On two units in same province?

1) general rule: All actions include an opposed DRN. Penetration versus MR. damage versus protection. Attack versus defence. Magic spells do different kinds of damage. So how they penetrate depends on damage type. Elemental damage can be 100% resisted. But any damage that penetrates resistance then goes against protection. Unless a spell is Armour Negating. Then protection is effectively zero. Best option is to carefully read the spell description. There are some weird ones like magma. And stellar cascades does fatigue damage but does have to overcome protection. So while it's 25 fatigue damage, it does very little versus a protection 25 dude.

2) If you have a magic weapon then yes, all damage penetrates ethereal.

3) Death bless adds 2 AN damage to your attacks and counts as magic.

4) Items of the same type do not stack on one commander. The exception is bracers of protection. Two commanders with a bane venom each should stack. But bane venom is a weird thing. There are folks who know better exactly how it works. From my experience it's of moderate value and very slow to do anything. I used to use it a lot and now rarely bother. A black servant with a skeleton talisman is cheaper and can kill low PD. Beats just killing the population. Most folks put bane venoms on black servants but there are much better options. For the same gem cost put on a skeleton amulet and any cheap armour and you have a fine stealthy light thug.
