1 How does temperature affect mages ?

Increased enc. (added to fatigue) when casting spells on the battlefield (+2 enc. in extreme cold or heat only, if not immune, or +3 enc for every cold level if cold-blooded). Cold-blooded units have even a small +1 penalty in cold/heat 0 area.

Example : in a +3 cold province, a cold-blooded lamia queen has an enc. of 15 (base 4 + 9 for the extreme cold) and a Mystic has an enc. of 6 (base 4 + 2 for the cold). In a +2 heat province, they have no penalty.


2 
Magic -0.5 MR of units in province (rounded down), 10% less fatigue from casting, +0.5 points research (rounded up)
Drain +0.5 MR of units in province (rounded down), 10% more fatigue from casting, -0.5 points research (rounded up) 



3 What effects does a drain dominion have other than lower research and higher magic resistance ?

Increased fatigue when casting spells on the battlefield (+10 % fat for every drain level, or -10 % for every positive magic level in the province). Example : in a +2 magic province, Marignon Initiates can cast Will-o-Wisp for 84 fat. only (base 100, -20%, +4 enc.), in a -2 drain the same spell makes 124 fatigue points (base 100, +20%, +4 enc.).

4 Some battle spell are affected by scales.

Grip of Winter and Heat from Hell are affected by heat/cold

Water element becomes Ice element in cold province

QUOTE (Micah @ Jul 24 2012, 10:40 AM)
IIRC resistance suffices to stop the extreme heat or cold penalty, you don't need full immunity.

You need 50% resistance to negate the extreme heat/cold penalty. 25-40% resistance gives you +1 encumbrance. The distinction does not matter that much, as only like 4 units have FR that is between 0 and 50%, and all of those are Gath recruitables.

Vulnerability to fire/cold does not increase encumbrance.

-> I tested the iceprot + items with a LA caelum storm general, who gets +2 prot per cold scale. The general keeps his iceprot boost regardless of what armour you give him. So for example if you give him a black steel plate he has 29 body prot in cold 3.

The rime hauberk stacks with this, so if you give storm general one he now has iceprot 3 and gets +9 prot to both head and body in cold 3. Which, in case he wears only the rime hauberk, adds up to head: 27 and body: 24.

A unit without iceprot given the rime hauberk will get iceprot 1 for head protection as well, even without a helmet.

In case anyone was wondering, bracers of protection does not scale this up like they do with earth bless, you only get the normal 3 extra prot.
